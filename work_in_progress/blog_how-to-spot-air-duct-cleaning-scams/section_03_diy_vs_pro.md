## When Should Rockford Homeowners Call a Professional vs. DIY?

You can safely handle basic duct maintenance yourself, but anything requiring access inside the duct system needs a certified professional. Here is where the line falls.

### Safe checks you can do yourself

- **Inspect your registers** - remove covers and look inside the boot with a flashlight; visible debris clinging to the duct wall is a sign cleaning is overdue
- **Check your filters** - a filter that loads within two weeks is a signal that particulate concentration is high in your system
- **Smell the supply vents when the furnace runs** - musty or dusty odor during first heat of the season is a meaningful indicator
- **Look for visible mold near return air grilles** - surface mold at a return grille can indicate a deeper moisture problem worth investigating

### Red flags that require a licensed expert in Rockford

- **Carbon or soot staining** around supply registers - indicates combustion gases entering the duct system, a safety issue beyond cleaning
- **Collapsed or disconnected flex duct** - visible only with a camera; requires physical repair before cleaning
- **Confirmed water intrusion in plenum** - requires professional remediation and source inspection, not just cleaning
- **HVAC system performance decline after prior duct cleaning** - may indicate the previous cleaner damaged internal components or disconnected a branch
- **Visible black growth on plenum surfaces** - requires laboratory identification before treatment; never treat assumed mold without testing

Our Story: How Rockford Air Duct Pros Came to Serve Winnebago County

Founded in Rockford in 2011, Rockford Air Duct Pros was established to close a critical gap in regional indoor air quality engineering. While legacy cleaning outfits relied on underpowered portable shop-vacs that merely aerosolized fine particulate back into living quarters, our engineering team invested in high-vacuum negative pressure equipment and sealed containment infrastructure.

Over the past 15 years, our dispatch terminal on North Main Street has expanded from single-residence remediations along the Rock River corridor to servicing major manufacturing complexes, municipal healthcare facilities, and commercial campuses throughout Northern Illinois. Every service vehicle operates under strict NADCA ACR standards with zero cross-contamination protocols.

Can commercial HVAC duct cleaning be performed during off-hours or weekends?

Yes. We routinely schedule commercial duct remediation during evening hours, overnight shifts, and weekends. We coordinate directly with your facility management or building security personnel to execute all containment, mechanical scrubbing, and cleanup while your building is unoccupied, ensuring zero disruption to normal business operations.

How often should Rockford commercial facilities clean their ventilation ductwork?

According to NADCA and ASHRAE recommendations, commercial office buildings should receive duct inspections every two years and comprehensive cleaning every three to four years. Manufacturing plants, healthcare clinics, and food preparation facilities typically require annual or biennial cleaning due to heavier airborne particulate loads.

Does commercial duct cleaning disrupt daily tenant activities or create indoor dust?

No. Our negative air containment systems maintain continuous negative pressure throughout the isolated duct zone during cleaning. High-efficiency HEPA filtration ensures dislodged particulates, scale, and dust are drawn directly into sealed vacuum units, preventing airborne contamination from entering office suites or retail sales floors.
